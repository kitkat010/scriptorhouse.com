Mike McCarthy a day ago
★★★★★

"Endorsements come and go, as do those folks who can sell ice to Eskimos. But having gratitude, and integrity sticks like glue, for the long run, and that is what an author needs and finds in doing business with Scryptor LLC and Scriptorhouse Publishing. I was lucky to be found by Scryptor and their Copyright Coordinator. I had gone the circuit with two publishing firms promising the world but delivering very little. No gratitude, no integrity just trying to take advantage of someone not knowing their industry. I truly felt my books and stories had more value and meaning then they were doing to market them. And I did everything I could. After 7 phone conversations with my copyright coordinator I was convinced this firm was different. I wanted someone who was an individual of high integrity and in Scryptor and Scriptorhouse Publishing, I found one. He would listen fully to my comments and concerns, he heard my voice and felt my own passion, I really began to see him differently, and I began to trust him. Like life itself, to find a real partner for a budding author is a key. It's a lonely life without one. In most cases as new authors, we are learning while we are running the race. I can't say enough about the professionalism, the teamwork involved, and how truly he enjoys doing work on my behalf. My bottom line is this — I enjoy writing and he is a cheerleader on my behalf, I mean, how great is that. And because of him and a film acquisition director he put me in contact with I just completed my third book, and began laying out the outline for my 4th. It's still about gratitude and integrity, you would be wise to check out, Scryptor and Scriptorhouse Publishing. Good luck on your journey!"
