Notice Regarding Impersonation, Unauthorized Representation, and False Affiliations

Scriptor House LLC • Scriptor House Films
IMPORTANT NOTICE

Introduction

Scriptor House LLC and Scriptor House Films are issuing this public notice to address an increasing number of instances involving companies, agencies, organizations, and individuals who are presenting themselves as, or creating the impression that they are affiliated with, Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films.

We have become aware of multiple instances of unauthorized representation and impersonation involving our company names, identity, services, business relationships, and professional activities.

SPECIFIC NOTICE

The Adaptation Center

NOT AFFILIATED

The Adaptation Center is NOT Scriptor House LLC and is NOT Scriptor House Films.

One entity that has come to our attention is The Adaptation Center. We wish to make the following clear:

  • The Adaptation Center should not represent itself as Scriptor House, Scriptor House Films, an official division or department of either organization, or an authorized representative acting on behalf of either organization.
  • Scriptor House LLC and Scriptor House Films have not authorized The Adaptation Center to represent itself as either entity or to create the impression that communications, services, proposals, agreements, or business dealings conducted through The Adaptation Center originate from or are officially endorsed by Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films.
  • Any similarity in services, terminology, industry language, or references to adaptation, publishing, film, or entertainment does not establish an affiliation with Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films.
GENERAL WARNING

Other Unauthorized Representations

The Adaptation Center is not the only entity involved in representations that may cause confusion. We are also aware of other parties that have used or referenced the Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films identity in ways that may lead members of the public to believe they are officially connected to our organizations.

Companies Agencies Websites Social Media Accounts Consultants Representatives Individuals

Some may use similar names, descriptions of services, company information, project references, personnel names, branding elements, or other identifying information to create an appearance of affiliation.

Unless expressly authorized by Scriptor House LLC or Scriptor House Films, such representations are not official and should not be relied upon as communications or commitments from our organizations.

OFFICIAL CHANNELS

Official Correspondence and Communications

To protect our authors, clients, filmmakers, partners, and members of the public from confusion, please be advised that official correspondence from Scriptor House LLC and Scriptor House Films should come through our authorized company communication channels and representatives.

If you receive a message from someone claiming to represent Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films, but the communication does not come through an authorized company channel, we recommend verifying the person's relationship with us before proceeding. This applies regardless of how the claimed relationship is described, including claims of being an employee, representative, agent, partner, consultant, affiliate, division, department, or other authorized party.

FOR THE RECORD

Our Official Identity

For the avoidance of doubt, the following are the two official entities operating under the Scriptor House name:

SCRIPTOR HOUSE LLC

The publishing company operating under the Scriptor House name, providing professional publishing support, author development, and creative services.

SCRIPTOR HOUSE FILMS

The film division operating under the Scriptor House Films name, focused on story-to-screen development and film production pathways.

Neither organization has authorized unrelated third parties to present themselves as Scriptor House, Scriptor House Films, or an official division, department, extension, partner, or representative of our organizations without express authorization.

The Adaptation Center is a separate entity and should not represent itself as Scriptor House, Scriptor House Films, or an authorized representative of either organization. Likewise, any other company, agency, organization, or individual making a similar claim without authorization should not be considered an official representative of Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films.

OUR COMMITMENT

Protecting Our Clients and Community

We value the trust placed in Scriptor House and Scriptor House Films by our authors, clients, filmmakers, partners, and professional community.

This notice is being issued to eliminate confusion and to help ensure that individuals who wish to work with us can clearly distinguish official Scriptor House / Scriptor House Films communications from communications made by separate and unauthorized entities.

If you encounter a website, email, proposal, social media account, advertisement, company, agency, or individual claiming an affiliation with Scriptor House or Scriptor House Films, please contact us directly so that the claim can be verified.

WHEN IN DOUBT,
VERIFY BEFORE PROCEEDING.
